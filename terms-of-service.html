2. How the conversation works

  • You text what you are hiring for. We ask follow-ups and send back a short synthesis so you can confirm we understood.
  • If you choose to connect Google Workspace, that connection is read-only. We can look at signals that help us understand your work context and hiring conversations. We do not send email, create calendar events, change files, or otherwise take actions in your account.
  • We may then send anonymized talent cards. Cards do not reveal a person’s real name. Names you see on cards are synthesized for the demo, and photos may be AI-generated and blurred.
  • Reacting with “?” on a card can unlock a short bio of who that person is and why they fit — still without revealing their real name.
