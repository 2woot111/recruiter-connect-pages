This Privacy Policy explains what Series Inc. (“Series,” “we,” “us”) collects when you use Series Recruiter (Demo) at +1 (415) 323-8520, how we use it, and the choices you have. It works together with our Terms of Service.

1. Information we collect

  • Your phone number and messages. When you text the demo, we receive your number and whatever you send, including what you are hiring for (role, seniority, must-haves, location, team, and follow-ups).
  • Conversation state. We keep a short record of where you are in the demo (for example, whether we have confirmed the role or whether you connected Workspace) so the agent can continue the thread.
  • Google Workspace, only if you consent and connect. If you check the consent box and complete the Google connection (through Composio), we receive read-only access. That may include signals from your work life — such as hiring-related conversations or calendar context — used to understand who is in your orbit. We do not use this access to send email, create events, or change anything in your account.
  • Technical logs. Our systems may log delivery status, timestamps, and errors needed to run the iMessage demo.

2. How we use information

  • To run the conversation: ask what you are hiring for, synthesize it, and confirm we understood.
  • To show how Series sources people by relating who you know to who Series knows, and to send anonymized talent cards that match the search.
  • If you connected Workspace, to read work-context signals that help surface relevant people — not to act in your account.
  • To operate, debug, and improve the demo.

3. Anonymized talent cards

When we showcase talent, we do not reveal a person’s real name. Display names on cards are synthesized for the demo. Photos may be AI-generated and blurred. A “?” reaction can return a short bio that still does not include the real name. Please do not try to re-identify people from these cards.

4. Who we share information with

  • Composio, to create and manage the optional Google connection you approve.
  • Google, when you authorize Workspace access through that flow.
  • Language-model providers we use to generate replies and summaries of what you told us you are hiring for.
  • Infrastructure providers that deliver iMessage on our behalf (including Linq).

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your chat or Workspace data to advertise to you.

5. How long we keep it

We keep demo conversation data and connection status as long as needed to run this demonstration and troubleshoot it, then delete or de-identify it. You can ask us to delete your demo data by emailing sean@series.so.

6. Your choices

  • You can use the chat without connecting Google Workspace.
  • You must actively agree on the sign-up / consent page before we send you to connect.
  • Text STOP to opt out of further messages from this number.
  • You can revoke Google access at any time in your Google account’s third-party app settings.
  • Email sean@series.so to ask what we have about you or to request deletion.

7. Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect demo data. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and this is a demonstration environment.

8. Children

The demo is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Changes

If we update this policy, we will change the effective date above. Material changes will be reflected on this page before they apply to new use of the demo.

10. Contact

Privacy questions: sean@series.so.
